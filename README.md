### Hi there 👋

I'm Manuel Etchegaray, a software developer from Argentina :shipit:

I build peer-to-peer, self-hosted software, and I'm a crypto and web3 enthusiast at heart. Decentralize everything! :collision:

🔭 **Open source projects**
- [ToolDB](https://github.com/Manwe-777/tool-db): a decentralized database for peer-to-peer apps that runs anywhere, with no central servers, now powered by Nostr.
- [MTG Arena Tool](https://github.com/mtgatool/mtgatool-desktop): a collection browser, deck tracker and stats manager for Magic: The Gathering Arena, available as a desktop overlay or in the browser.
- [PID Tuna](https://github.com/Manwe-777/pid-tuna): a blackbox log analyzer for tuning quadcopters. A free, web-based alternative to PIDtoolbox that's faster, cross-platform and mobile friendly.
- [Rulebook](https://github.com/Manwe-777/rulebook): an open, decentralized Warhammer 40k rules viewer and list builder with automatic validation and sharing, built on ToolDB and Nostr.
- [EDH Hub](https://github.com/Manwe-777/edh-hub): a smooth, lightweight life counter for EDH. Native on Android, PWA everywhere else.
- [Toolnet](https://github.com/Manwe-777/toolnet): a self-hosted, Tailscale-like private mesh VPN powered by ToolDB, with no limits.

🏠 **Homelab & owning my software**
- [Psysonic-hub](https://github.com/ImAsra/psysonic-hub) & [Psysonic-headless](https://github.com/Manwe-777/psysonic-headless): home integration for [Psysonic](https://github.com/Psysonic/psysonic), a hi-fi desktop music player inspired by good ol' Winamp, with Navidrome/Subsonic support.
- [homelab-monitor](https://github.com/Manwe-777/homelab-monitor): monitor your homelab apps from anywhere, with phone notifications when something dies and a self-hosted status page. Powered by ToolDB.
